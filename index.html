About these zmanim

Each colored lane is an allowed window: earliest at one end, latest at the other. Times come from HebCal for the selected city and date. Hover a lane or click it for the clock times.

Tallit & Tefillin
Earliest: Misheyakir (sun 11.5° below the horizon). Latest: Sof Zman Tefillah GRA (end of the morning Shacharit window).
Shema (MGA)
Earliest: Alot HaShachar (dawn, 16.1°). Latest: Sof Zman Shema Magen Avraham (end of the 3rd morning hour on the MGA clock).
Shema (GRA)
Earliest: Netz (sunrise). Latest: Sof Zman Shema GRA (end of the 3rd morning hour from sunrise to sunset).
Shacharit
Earliest: Netz. Latest: Sof Zman Tefillah GRA (end of the 4th morning hour).
Mincha Gedola
Earliest: Mincha Gedola (half an hour after midday). Latest: Shkia (sunset).
Mincha Ketana
Earliest: Mincha Ketana (9.5 halachic hours after sunrise). Latest: Shkia.
Plag HaMincha
Earliest: Plag (10.75 hours after sunrise). Latest: Tzet (nightfall). Lighting Shabbat early is only after Plag.
Maariv
Earliest: Tzet (~7.083°). Latest shown: Tzet 72 (72 minutes after sunset, Rabbeinu Tam).
Shabbat & Yom Tov candles
Shown only on Erev Shabbat or Erev Yom Tov.
Earliest: Plag HaMincha (you may light early and accept Shabbat/Yom Tov).
Latest: HebCal candle-lighting (18 minutes before sunset by default; 40 in Jerusalem, 30 in Haifa / Zikhron Yaakov).
After Shabbat (Yom Tov that starts Saturday night): the window starts at tzet; latest is HebCal’s post-Shabbat lighting time.

These times are approximations for learning and planning. Ask a local posek for halacha lemaaseh. Candle-lighting and zmanim follow HebCal; location is a GeoNames city (population ≥ 1000).
